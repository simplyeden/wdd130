Raft and Dine

Drift across the river on a gentle rafting journey, perfect for families and all ages. Spend 1–2 hours enjoying the water, then savor a one-hour brunch at our scenic waterside restaurant. A relaxing blend of adventure and dining in one all-inclusive package.

The Rapid Adventure

Enjoy a two-hour, high-energy rafting experience with the Rapid Adventure. Race through wild rapids and test your skills in competitive challenges. Exclusively for guests 18 and older, perfect thrill seeking for groups or individuals who crave adrenaline.

The Adventure Combo

Experience a full day of the ultimate adventure with our all-inclusive adventure combo. Begin with a scenic hike to a breathtaking hilltop, dive from cliffs into refreshing waters, and raft your way to our riverside restaurant for a delicious lunch. Afterward, unwind with a lazy river float, letting the current carry you into pure relaxation.
