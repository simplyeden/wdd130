White Water Rafting Company Ltd

Your happy place for fun, laughter, adrenaline, and relaxation. Our aim is for the entire family to have a grand time. Whether you want to relax in nature or pump up your adrenaline, we got you!. White Water Rafting has all the wholesome activity to suit your needs.

History

White Water Rafting was founded in 1998. We wanted a place that combined nature with fun. White Water Rafting started with our Lazy River Raft offering relaxation for the family.

Later in 2001, the hike raft was added as a fun adventure for hiking lovers. Today White Water Rafting is still the ideal place that could pump up the adrenaline and also offer relation.
